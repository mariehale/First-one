## Nav bar
- Logo/wordmark: **The Honey Method**
- Button: **Get in touch** → links to the referral section / booking calendar

## Hero
- Eyebrow: `HOME ORGANIZING & RESALE, REFERRAL ONLY`
- Headline: **No shame. Just *space*.**
- Subhead: "A mother-daughter team that clears the clutter, builds a system you'll actually keep, and sells what you don't need — without a single judgmental word."
- Button 1 (primary): **Book a discovery call** → Discovery Walkthrough calendar link
- Button 2 (ghost): **See how it works** → jumps to How It Works section

## Who's showing up at your door
- Headline: **Marie & Honey**
- Body: "We're a mom-and-daughter team. Marie runs the quotes, scheduling, and the tricky conversations. Honey — a freshman with an eye for detail and a genuine gift for structure — handles every photo and keeps each job moving the exact same way, every time."
- Body 2: "That consistency isn't an accident. It's what makes the work good: the same room-by-room process, the same gentle questions, the same care with things that are hard to let go of."
- Side card: two-line bios —
  - **Marie** — Quotes, planning, the hard conversations
  - **Honey** — Photos, systems, keeping every visit on track

## How it works (4 steps)
1. **Walkthrough** — "A free look at the space and an honest conversation about what's actually driving you crazy."
2. **Your plan** — "A written room-by-room plan with a real time estimate — no vague 'it depends.'"
3. **The work** — "Keep, Sell, Donate, Toss. Everything gets a home. Donations leave the same day."
4. **Your workbook** — "A short, encouraging recap after every visit — what's done, what's next, and one thing worth noticing about yourself."

## Starter pricing (3 cards)
- **Quick Refresh** — $150 — "One small space (closet, pantry, one cabinet). 2–3 hours. Full sort, clean, and label."
- **Room Reset** (mark as "Most booked") — $275 — "One full room. 4–5 hours. Written plan + progress workbook."
- **Hourly** — $60/hr, team of 2 — "Whole-home or unusual spaces. 3-hour minimum. Custom multi-visit plan."
