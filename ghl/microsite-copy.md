# Microsite Copy — Honey!

Build this as a one-page site in GHL's site/funnel builder (or "AI Studio" if it gets you a faster starting layout — either way, use this copy, not whatever the AI generates by default). Design reference with the full visual layout: **https://claude.ai/artifact/HsUr1YzFYwB7BFtJJKTvV5**

**Images needed:** the mockup uses a CSS-drawn honey jar since there's no real photo yet. Swap in either (a) a real photo of Honey and Marie, or (b) a simple purchased/free icon of a honey jar or organized shelf. Don't leave the CSS placeholder on the live site.

**Voice:** playful, fierce, warm — see `BRAND.md` for the full tagline hierarchy and signature concepts. The site is exactly the kind of brand-facing material that gets full personality (unlike transactional booking texts).

- Logo/wordmark: **Honey!**

## Hero
- Eyebrow: `HOME ORGANIZING & RESALE, REFERRAL ONLY`
- Headline: **Clutter, Meet Your Match.**
- Subhead: "Honey! No shame — we've got you. A mother-daughter team that clears the clutter, builds a system you'll actually keep, and sells what you don't need. We read the mess, never the person."
- Button 1 (primary): **The Library Is Open** → Discovery Walkthrough calendar link (this replaces a generic "Book a call" — it's the free, loving read of your space)
- Button 2 (ghost): **See how it works** → jumps to How It Works section

## Who's showing up at your door
- Headline: **Marie & Honey**
- Body: "We're a mom-and-daughter team. Marie runs the quotes, scheduling, and the tricky conversations. Honey — a freshman with an eye for detail and a genuine gift for structure — handles every photo and keeps each job moving the exact same way, every time."
- Body 2: "That consistency isn't an accident. It's what makes the work good: the same room-by-room process, the same loving reads, the same care with things that are hard to let go of."
- Side card: two-line bios —
  - **Marie** — Quotes, planning, the hard conversations
  - **Honey** — Photos, systems, keeping every visit on track

## How it works (4 steps)
1. **The Library Is Open** — "A free, loving read of your space and an honest conversation about what's actually driving you crazy."
2. **Your plan** — "A written room-by-room plan with a real time estimate — no vague 'it depends.'"
3. **Let's Werk This Room** — "Keep, Sell, Donate, Toss. Everything gets a home. Donations leave the same day."
4. **The Reveal** — "A short, encouraging workbook after every visit — what's done, what's next, and a before/after that gets its own spotlight."

## Starter pricing (3 cards)
- **Quick Refresh** — $150 — "One small space (closet, pantry, one cabinet). 2–3 hours. Full sort, clean, and label."
- **Room Reset** (mark as "Most booked") — $275 — "One full room. 4–5 hours. Written plan + progress workbook."
- **Hourly** — $60/hr, team of 2 — "Whole-home or unusual spaces. 3-hour minimum. Custom multi-visit plan."
