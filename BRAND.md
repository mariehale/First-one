# Brand Sheet

Everything in this repo (reports, workbooks, listings, emails, the GHL setup, the microsite) uses this so it all feels like one business, not scattered documents. Name and identity are confirmed — not a placeholder.

## Identity
- **Business name:** The Honey Method
- **Tagline:** No shame. Just space.
- **One-liner:** A mother-daughter team that organizes your home, sells what you don't need, and helps you let go of the guilt about how it got that way.
- **Voice (3 words):** Warm, direct, judgment-free.

## Voice rules (use everywhere — client emails, workbook, reports)
1. Never use "mess," "hoarding," "junk," or "should have." Use "space," "pile," "items," "what's here now."
2. Describe the *system*, not the person. "This closet doesn't have a home for shoes yet" not "you never put shoes away."
3. Lead with what got done before what's left.
4. Every client-facing document ends with one specific, genuine compliment about something they did that day (a decision they made, not just "great job").
5. See `sop/10-coaching-language-guide.md` for the full script library.
