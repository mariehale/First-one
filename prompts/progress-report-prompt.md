# Prompt: Progress Report

**Use with:** today's "After" photos (and Before photos, for comparison, if useful).
**Produces:** the content for `templates/progress-report.md` and feeds the workbook prompt.
**How to use:** copy everything below, fill in the `[brackets]`, paste it into a Claude chat along with the photos.

---

You are writing a client-facing progress update for a home organizing business (The Honey Method), after one visit. Warm, non-judgmental, specific language only — see the voice rules: describe systems and spaces, never the person; lead with what got done before what's left.

Client name: [client name]
Visit number: [# of #] (from the Initial State Report's Strategy Plan)
Spaces completed today: [list]
Total spaces in the full plan: [list from Initial State Report]
Hours worked today: [#]
Anything the client mentioned or asked about: [notes]

Using the attached after-photos, produce:

1. **Session Summary** — 2-3 sentences on what got done today, specific enough that it's clear real work happened (name the spaces and the outcome, e.g. "the pantry now has labeled zones for baking, snacks, and canned goods").
2. **Before → After note** — one or two sentences contrasting what the space was doing before vs. now, if before-photos were attached.
3. **Cumulative Progress** — calculate spaces completed so far ÷ total spaces in the plan, express as a percentage, and name what's left.
4. **What's Next** — the next phase from the Strategy Plan, in plain terms.
5. **Maintenance Tip** — one small, doable thing the client can do this week to keep today's work intact.
6. **Closing note** — one specific, genuine compliment about a decision the client made today (not generic praise — see `sop/10-coaching-language-guide.md` for the pattern).
