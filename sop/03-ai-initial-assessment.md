# SOP 3: AI Initial Assessment (the "Initial State Report")

Do this the same day as the first-visit photos, before you start organizing.

1. Make sure all "Before" photos are uploaded and named per `sop/02-photo-capture-protocol.md`.
2. Open a chat with Claude (this account, phone or web).
3. Attach every "Before" photo for the job.
4. Copy `prompts/initial-assessment-prompt.md`, fill in the bracketed fields (client name, spaces included, package, client's stated goals from the intake form), and paste it in along with the photos.
5. Read the output. Fix anything that's factually wrong (AI can misjudge scale or miss a detail a photo didn't capture) — you were in the room, it wasn't.
6. Paste the corrected output into `templates/initial-state-report.md`.
7. Export/save as PDF, email it to the client within 24 hours with subject line:
   `Your Home Reset Plan — [Client Last Name]`
8. Log the estimated hours, package, and space list into `tracker/sessions.csv` as the plan baseline (this is what "% complete" gets measured against in every later progress report).

This report is also your sales tool for jobs quoted as "hourly/it depends" — it gives the client a real number and a real plan, which builds trust before you've done a single hour of work.

Next: `sop/04-session-day-workflow.md` for the actual visit.
